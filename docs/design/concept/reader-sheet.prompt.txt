ONE production-style pixel sprite concept sheet, four frames in ONE horizontal row, evenly spaced, each frame based on a tiny 32x32 pixel grid, enlarged with nearest-neighbor so pixels are LARGE, square, unmistakable. This must look like a hand-placed 32-pixel videogame sprite, NOT an illustration rendered with small jagged edges. Use ONLY FOUR opaque flat colors: outline #2f2a3d, sage #6faa76, cream #fffaf0, gold #a8741a. Background: true transparent alpha; absolutely no checkerboard, white matte, scenery, platform, text, grid lines, labels or frame borders. Character: small gender-neutral unnamed explorer for ages 10-12; short sage cape, simple cream tunic and trousers, small blank-card pouch, no hat, no facial gender markers, no weapon. Right-facing three-quarter full-body silhouette consistent across all 4 frames, with simple dark eye pixel and rounded proportions. Frames left to right: 1) standing idle, blank card held near chest. 2) defense, spreading several blank cards into a small fan, feet stable. 3) question attack, pointing with an empty hand toward the right at an implied sentence, other hand holds blank card; no physical strike or projectile. 4) defeat, knee bent, head gently lowered, blank cards placed near feet, no injury. Identical character identity, head size, palette, baseline, and frame scale. Keep every body, hand, cape, and card inside its separate cell. No lettering on the blank cards. Leave clear transparent gutters between the four poses. Crisp 1-logical-pixel outline, a few big solid blocks, no extra shading colors, no antialiasing, no blur, no gradient, no glossy lighting. Horizontal sheet. Original character, no recognizable existing game or commercial sprite reference.
