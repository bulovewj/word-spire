ONE horizontal sprite concept sheet with FIVE evenly separated frames, all on true transparent alpha. A tiny original hollow flower-ring paper creature facing LEFT for a cozy Korean children's literacy game. Hand-placed 32x32 pixel grid per frame enlarged into chunky sharp square pixels. Use ONLY ink outline #2f2a3d and THREE flat body colors: sage #6faa76, cream #fffaf0, lavender #7a7393. No shading colors, gradients, antialiasing, blur, glow or texture. The silhouette is a rounded scalloped SAGE FLOWER RING with a clearly open circular center; the center MUST BE COMPLETELY TRANSPARENT, NOT dark paint. A small cream fog tail hangs below the ring. Two short curled cream PAPER RIBBONS hang as arms, not weapons. Two small lavender eyes sit on the lower outer ring, not floating in the empty hole. Friendly and oddly cute; no scary face, teeth, horns, leaves, real bee or wings. Frames left to right: idle; attack by gently curving its two paper ribbons outward without contact; sealing stage 1 flower ring folds vertically like paper; sealing stage 2 becomes a small folded cream slip; sealing stage 3 the slip tucks into a simple sage bookmark with ink outline. Keep the first two character sizes and baselines identical. Sealing becomes progressively smaller. Keep each frame within its cell, with transparent gutters. No scenery, floor, shadows, checkerboard, white matte, grid lines, labels, letters, numbers, words, title or existing game visual designs. Wide horizontal composition, simple large readable blocks.
